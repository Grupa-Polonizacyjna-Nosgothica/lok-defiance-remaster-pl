Now, at last,
the masks had fallen away.
The strings of the puppets had become visible,
and the hands of the prime mover
exposed.
Most ironic of all
was the last gift
that Raziel had given me:
More powerful than the sword that
now held his soul,
more acute even than the vision
his sacrifice had accorded me.
The first,
bitter taste of that terrible illusion:
Hope.
