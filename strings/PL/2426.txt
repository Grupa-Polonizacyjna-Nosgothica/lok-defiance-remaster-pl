Introduced: Legacy of Kain: Soul Reaver (1999)

The second of the six lieutenants to be sired by Kain some five centuries after the destruction of the Pillars, Turel fought alongside Kain and Raziel in the subsequent vampire conquest of Nosgoth, and endured as third-in-command of Kain's empire for almost a millennium.

Turel, Raziel and their fellow clan leaders reigned over the world in a brotherly rivalry for centuries upon end. But, when Raziel dramatically transgressed against Kain, and was ordered to be executed, Turel personally cast him into the Abyss - apparently without question - and assumed his brother's place as their master's right hand.

As the centuries passed, Turel - like his fellow surviving vampire lieutenants - began to mutate and shift from his humanoid form. Turel and his brood became bat-like in appearance, bearing large ears with incredible powers of hearing at the price of faltering vision.

Through the use of a time-streaming device stolen from Moebius, Azimuth the Planer - the possessed Guardian of Dimension - summoned Turel from the future back to the darkness of the Avernus Catacombs, exploited as an immortal mouthpiece by the Hylden in order to further their final cause of toppling the Pillars.

Having failed to locate Turel and exact his revenge in previous adventures, Raziel happens upon his long-lost brother, monstrously devolved, in a pit strewn with countless blood sacrifices. Now known as Hash'ak'gik, the Blood God of Avernus, Turel has long been venerated by the Cenobites that walk these dark halls - but seems more a deranged, victimized captive than an impressive deity.
