This second type of Archon (the "Dreadnaught") is larger and stronger than its predecessor. One might describe them as the 'heavy infantry' of the Archon world. Due to their considerable size and lack of speed, they prefer to stay close to the ground, while still exhibiting the same sinuous swimming motions the Reaper Archons do.

Projectile Hold: The Dreadnaught Archon's primary form of attack, other than swiping with their massive hooklike arms, is a projectile of spectral ichor. This webbing, if well-aimed, will cause the struck target to become immobilized. The Dreadnaught Archon will then approach the victim and attempt to slam its massive claws into their vulnerable flesh. Conversely, if the opponent is not held, the Archon will tend to hang back, away from their prey, until a projectile attack is successful.

Draining Leash: The Dreadnaught Archon's second form of attack is an energy-draining leash, very similar to that of the Reaper Archon. This can be initiated by a successful melee strike, but is most often used in conjunction with the Projectile Hold. As their prey will be completely vulnerable during the Hold, the Dreadnaught is free for a moment to initiate this health-draining ability without resistance. Once the Draining Leash is latched on, the Archon will back off in an attempt to protect this connection.
