Introduced: Blood Omen: Legacy of Kain (1996)

After the vampire purges, Vorador - disgusted with humanity and human affairs - retreated to this remote Mansion deep within the Termogent Forest, a treacherous, near-impassable swamp which has effectively kept vampire hunters away, but which vampires can deftly traverse using their abilities.

The expansive Mansion is opulent, even decadent - more a castle, in truth, including the undercrofts beneath. The many floors and areas within are hung with tapestries, curtains of velvet, plush rugs. Low, sparse candlelight illuminates a bevy of murals, paintings and statues memorializing the triumphs of the vampire race.

Yet where, precisely, has the elusive Vorador ensconced himself?
