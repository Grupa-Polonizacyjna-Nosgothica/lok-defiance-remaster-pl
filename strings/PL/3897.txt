EARLY USCHTENHEIM

The last of the Lost Levels we present to you in Defiance Remastered, this prototypical, lower-fidelity incarnation of the village of Uschtenheim also has unusual provenance. It originates not from Defiance, but from the rare, early builds of Soul Reaver 2 (2001) - prior to that game's shift to more powerful contemporary hardware.

While not apparent here, some early 3D interpretations of Uschtenheim positioned Janos Audron's Retreat (then "Janos's House") within the hamlet itself - in alignment with design material from Blood Omen: Legacy of Kain (1996).
