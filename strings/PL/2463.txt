This variant of abyssal Demon has apparently been spawned from the fiery inferno of a black and hopeless alternate dimension. Preferring a close range, this gargantuan will rush up and wield its thunderous strength against its targeted prey. Once at close range, the Demon's options of attack are many in number, and all brutal.

Breath of the Inferno: Opening its massive fanged maw, the Demon can unleash a torrent of blazing fire before it. This is a close-to-medium range attack designed for area of effect. Seeing the windup for this attack, the victim can quickly dodging around behind the occupied Demon to evade.
