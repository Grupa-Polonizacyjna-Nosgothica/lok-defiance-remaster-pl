The Soul Reaver is the sword that the Reaver is fated to become. In its future, a ravenous spirit will be consumed by - and imprisoned within - its shell, transforming it from a blood-drinking blade to a soul-stealing weapon.

Kain first discovered the Soul Reaver as a fledgling vampire, the sword having been planted in Avernus Cathedral by Moebius for him to discover. The unique nature of the Soul Reaver enabled Moebius to manipulate Kain into altering history for his own ends - a power which Kain has studied and learned to turn against Moebius over the centuries, in his own, equally calculated bid to master destiny.

Having wielded the Soul Reaver for aeons, Kain fatefully raised it against Raziel in their confrontation at the Pillars. The ancient blade, believed to be indestructible, irrevocably shattered against Raziel when Kain attempted to strike him down. Kain, for his part, seemed not stunned, but strangely satisfied with this shocking outcome...

...for it confirmed that the soul captive within the blade had been none other than Raziel's, unable to devour itself.
