Though a comparatively young (and once-insignificant) species, humans have risen to become the current dominant civilization in Nosgoth. Like the contemporary generation of vampires, both Kain and Raziel originated as humans.

Despite their relative frailty, Nosgoth's human population has turned a tenacious will to survive (and sheer strength in numbers) to their advantage over the centuries, having forcefully seized control of the Pillars and the Circle of Nine from the Vampires who originated them.

In recent history, the humans' territorial dominion has continued to expand, and feudal divisions have gradually fallen by the wayside in lieu of an impetus to greater unity. Consequently, their persecution of the vampire race - their collective enemy - has proven increasingly effective.
