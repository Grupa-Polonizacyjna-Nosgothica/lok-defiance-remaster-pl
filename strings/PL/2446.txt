Once reigning over the limitless power of thought, consciousness and emotion, the original Vampire Guardian of Mind now rests as but a mere specter within the Citadel.

Mental Simulacrum: This ability is her main offense and defense. Casting this spell will cause her to vanish and reappear with multiple illusions of herself about the room. Each illusion will have only one hit point, and dissipates with a single attack. If Raziel strikes the 'real' Guardian, they will all display the same hit reaction and dissipate instantly.
