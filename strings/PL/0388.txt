THE AIR FORGE

After the decision was taken to include all seven Reaver Forges in Defiance - but before the redesign which resulted in their consolidation into similar chambers at the Vampire Citadel - the team experimented with direct adaptation of the Forge assets from Soul Reaver 2 (2001).

This incomplete take on the Air Forge is effectively a remix of its predecessor, retaining the architectural and textural stylings of its Soul Reaver 2 counterpart, but virtually nothing of its layout. Unlike the Water Forge, no design documentation for this area is known to have been preserved, if any was created.

The Forge consists of a short series of corridors which branch out into two parts, one leading to an exterior room, and the other to a smaller-scale version of the giant snake sculpture in the previous game, surrounded by three god-heads.

The test area is clearly unfinished, leading to a black void which serves as a dead end, and it is unclear where Raziel would have forged the Reaver. Only the Dark Forge is known to have received similar adaptation.
