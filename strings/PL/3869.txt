AVERNUS - STREETS

The largest city in Nosgoth, Avernus was introduced in Blood Omen: Legacy of Kain (1996). Envisaged as a major hub area for Soul Reaver 2 (2001), it was quickly cut for reasons of scope, and while its central Cathedral eventually made its way into Defiance, the city beyond suffered a similar fate.

Created by artist Darren McKinsey, this street is the last surviving fragment of what was pictured as a huge cityscape. Kain and Raziel would need to navigate the chaos of its burning ruins in pursuit of their goals, with Kain encountering and defeating (but not slaying) his old foe, Azimuth the Planer, in his side of the story.
