With the vampire population having dwindled to near-extinction, and with heavy losses on both sides, the Sarafan were gradually disbanded - but these old wounds represented naught but mere failure to a renewed and rising vampire-hunting regime formed in answer to King William the Just's assassination some centuries later, with Moebius himself at its helm.

In the era of Moebius's genocidal vampire purge - the time of Kain's youth - hunters flocked to serve as soldiers in the Time Streamer's mercenary army. Unlike the Sarafan who preceded them, the vampire hunters were more cutthroat militia than orderly army - but what they lacked in organization they more than made up for in zealous brutality.

By the time Kain joined its number, the vampire race had been driven to the brink of extinction once more. Their impaled corpses littered the land, and Vorador, their eldest surviving patriarch, was among the last of his kind.
