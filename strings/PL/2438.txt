This specter, the original Vampire Guardian of Dimension, long ago served the Pillars by controlling and defining the very boundaries, consistency and form of space itself.

Spatial Rift: This ability is a basic teleport that severs as an offensive and defensive maneuver. It can be used defensively to evade the Reaver's range, or to move in close and attack.

Blast of Distortion: This is a very quick guided energy missile.
