THE EARLY SARAFAN STRONGHOLD

In early production on Defiance, the Sarafan Stronghold adhered to a design much more akin to its Soul Reaver 2 (2001) incarnation.

This prior iteration of the Stronghold, by Steve Goss, provides a ready touchpoint with Soul Reaver 2 not only in terms of its floor plan, but in its sense of scale. It features the first versions of several common landmarks carried over into the final level, such as the Bridge area and the Elder God Caverns, all much grander in size than the final, more compact vision of Defiance would support.

FALLEN ANGELS

This take on the Stronghold was constructed to accommodate not only Kain's first and third chapters, but also Raziel's, directly following on from the end of that game in the Chapter House room.

Some versions of the scenario featured a return to the ruined Stronghold in the near-future era previously explored in Soul Reaver 2, which would involve Kain infiltrating its confines to recover the head of Vorador.

Enjoy contrasting this version of the Stronghold with the final design. When you reach the Bridge, continue through to the Soul Reaver 2 Sanctuary to indulge your nostalgia, or fly to the left to explore the hidden depths!
