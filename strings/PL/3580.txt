After the Reaver's forging, the original Vampire weapon-masters constructed seven great elemental Reaver Forges for their champion to find, be tested by, and conquer. Each Forge was architected around the theme of a particular element. These ordeals were purpose-built to test the Reaver-bearer's unique abilities.

The elements are set in order by the nine principles represented by the Pillars, in a cosmogenic mandala. These forces are Air (governed by Mind and Dimension), Fire (Conflict and Nature), Earth (Energy and Time), and Water (States and Death). Air and Fire are aligned with Light, and Earth and Water are aligned with Darkness. Spirit, the central element, is associated with Balance.

The Forges were intended to imbue the physical Reaver blade with elemental powers. Though the ancient Vampires never foresaw nor conceived of the Reaver as a disembodied Wraith Blade, it fortunately matters not; they have the same effect as intended on Raziel's symbiotic weapon.
