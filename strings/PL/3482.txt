Raziel kneels prostrated before the paternalistic Elder God in the bowels of the Underworld, starving, suffering, in defeat and exhaustion.

Driven to the brink of oblivion when the Reaver almost consumed him, he has spent untold aeons as the Elder's captive here. For all this time, he has defied both the Elder's will, and his own inevitable future fate - to become the ravenous, soul-devouring entity imprisoned within the blade.

Ultimately, Raziel concludes it is better to face his destiny than cower from it, and ostensibly renews his dark covenant with his former master. But submission is not always what it seems...
