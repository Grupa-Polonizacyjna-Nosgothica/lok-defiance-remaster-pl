!!! SPOILER WARNING !!!

Please note that the Lost Levels contain behind-the-scenes commentaries encompassing the entirely of Legacy of Kain: Defiance, and are thus filled with significant spoilers in relation to its plot and visuals.

If you have not already experienced the main story of Defiance, we strongly recommend doing so before continuing your journey through the Lost Levels! These museum pieces are designed to enrich your understanding of the game and its world with greater context, but are best approached as a supper to enjoy upon devouring the main course. Although roleplaying a pair of ravenous vampires may be the object of this game, do not be so swift to slake your thirst for knowledge.

To accommodate more relaxed exploration, Kain and Raziel each come provided with all of the upgrades and abilities they would typically receive over the course of the main game. We have scattered a little fodder throughout the levels for you to amuse yourself with along the way, but fear not - these enemies cannot harm you, for you are also granted immunity to damage.
