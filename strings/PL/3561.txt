Over time, the original Vampires began to die out. The Pillars, with a will of their own, still called Guardians after the Vampires were gone - but now the Guardians born were human. The protection of the Pillars passed into human hands and human history - and their original Vampire origins were lost in the shrouds of myth and religion.

Only one Vampire survived and remained - the Tenth Guardian chosen for a sworn task (after the nine Pillars). The duty of the Tenth was to keep and guard the Reaver (the Vampire-forged weapon prophesied to be wielded by a champion against the Hylden) until the champion arrived to claim it. This Guardian - Janos Audron - waited faithfully for millennia, his hopes resting in the coming of this messiah.
