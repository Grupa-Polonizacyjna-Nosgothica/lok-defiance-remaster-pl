Despite his distrust of the mysterious so-called Oracle, Kain finds that its promises of delivering him in time prove true, and he emerges five centuries in the future.

Kain arrives at Avernus Cathedral, the seemingly peaceful heart of a city in chaos. He must find Raziel somewhere within - and force him to see reason. Only by preventing the resurrection of Janos Audron can he dare hope to see the Pillars and Nosgoth finally restored.

Will the prophesied heroes' battle commence, or can it yet be averted? And of both champions, who represents whom - the Vampire messiah, and the Hylden adversary?
