The flowing currents and shifting movements of time were kept in check long ago by this original Vampire Guardian.

Time Distortion: This spell is her main means of attack, taking the form of an alteration in the flow of time. Over the duration of this enchantment, time will speed up, reach an apex and then slow down back to normal. This is a global change for all parties in the room - even the Guardians.

Time Singularity: The effectiveness of this basic projectile attack is enhanced through the Guardian's Time Distortion.
