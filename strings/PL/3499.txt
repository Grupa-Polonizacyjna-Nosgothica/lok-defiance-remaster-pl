At the climax of the last great war, the Vampires pooled their collective magical energy to cast a massive collective spell, which resulted in the banishment of the Hylden away from the physical world - imprisoning them in the extra-dimensional Demon Realm.

The instrument of their banishment - the Binding - was the Pillars themselves. The Pillars were magically and instantaneously created as a physical manifestation of the Vampires' collective banishment-spell.

As the Hylden were shunted away into the Demon Realm, the nine self-generated Pillars of Nosgoth shot up from the bowels of the earth, up through the soil (and through the platform created by the Vampires for the Binding rite), and infinitely up to the heavens.
