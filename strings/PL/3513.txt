Though Nosgoth's history is essentially immutable...

...there is a loophole.

If a specific phenomenon can be arranged, it may displace history enough to change events - the threads of reality will endeavor to sew themselves up, such that no gaping paradoxes remain, but history will have been changed.

The necessary extraordinary occurrence is the convergence of the Soul Reaver with itself in time - if one can arrange for two incarnations of the Soul Reaver to appear at the same time and place, history can be altered.

And Raziel, as Reaver-incarnate, possesses a singularly unique nature - intertwined with his own future self, he alone is unbound by fate, the sole being empowered with the free will necessary to divert the time-stream.

Both Moebius and Kain know how to change history - that it requires a convergence of Soul Reavers in time - and realize the potential of using him to arrange these momentous incidents. Raziel, meanwhile, now realizes the power he wields, and seeks to take command of his own destiny...

The coin is still turning...
