Reaching infinitely into the sky and deep into the earth, the Pillars are a manifestation of the mysterious power that preserves and gives life to the Land of Nosgoth. The nine ancient Pillars represent the principles of Mind, Dimension, Conflict, Nature, Energy, Time, States, Death, and - at the center, binding them all together - Balance.

The Pillars stood like silent sentinels over Nosgoth since before the dawn of recorded history, and were perpetually represented by an oligarchy of sorcerer-guardians known as the Circle of Nine.

Each Guardian was marked from birth, chosen and summoned by the Pillar they were destined to serve for their lifetime. In return, the Guardians were granted longevity and extraordinary powers. The death of a Circle member was inevitably followed by the birth of a successor, whom it was the responsibility of the remaining Guardians to seek out and apprentice into the Circle.
