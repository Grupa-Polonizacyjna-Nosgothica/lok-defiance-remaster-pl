OVERLAND - SNOWY OVERLOOK

Working drafts of Defiance's world layout prescribed a more sprawling and interconnected overland, featuring several transitional locations which bookended more major areas. These open wildernesses included dense forests, mountain caverns, and even underwater ruins, continuing the series' tradition of a seamlessly-interconnected open world established in Blood Omen: Legacy of Kain (1996).

Several of these interstitials reached the production stage, though very little design documentation is known to have been archived. All were ultimately cut from the final game, and survive only in the form of distant vistas.
