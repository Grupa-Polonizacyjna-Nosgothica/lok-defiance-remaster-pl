THE GREAT SOUTHERN LAKE

Another transitional area made redundant early in the design phase, the Southern Lake makes its return from Soul Reaver 2 (2001), remodelled and significantly expanded with new and old paths to traverse.

While largely an untextured blockout, this incarnation of the Lake features some more elaborate additions, including a mysterious manse at the banks of the Stronghold dock, and several walled garrisons. Be on the lookout for the cut Theurgist enemy class, honing their skills at the Stronghold's gates... and rogue vampires lying in wait in the wilderness!
