His fury amplified by the influence of the Hylden, Raziel refuses to heed Kain's insistent warnings, and turns the tides of battle.

As he seizes a fatal advantage over his former master, the apocalyptic duel between both champions rages on. Determined to resurrect Janos, Raziel finds himself resorting to any means necessary to obtain the answers he has sought.

Though increasingly disillusioned - uninterested in saving the world - his character and his convictions will nevertheless be tested to the fullest extent as he uncovers Nosgoth's most deeply-buried secrets, and begins to confront his inescapable fate. The Soul Reaver's ultimate trial awaits...
