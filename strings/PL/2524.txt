Reaching infinitely into the sky and deep into the earth, the nine ancient Pillars have stood like silent sentinels over Nosgoth since before the dawn of recorded history.

The Pillars, more than just an edifice, are a manifestation of the mysterious power that preserves and gives life to Nosgoth - the spiritual and physical health of the land is tied inextricably to the Pillars' well-being.

It was here that Kain refused the sacrifice, and set his empire upon the ruined Pillars - in despair, but also in hope, that while he, the last Guardian, still lived, Balance might one day be restored.
