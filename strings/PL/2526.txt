The headquarters of the Sarafan crusade, the Stronghold was built on a promontory adjacent to the Great Southern Lake. Within the confines of this monastic fortress, the Sarafan warrior-priests live and train, and Moebius the Time Streamer guides and directs their actions.

While spartan and austere, the architecture of the Stronghold also celebrates the exploits of the Sarafan, glorified as near-angelic savior figures. Their prey, the vampires, are naturally demonized as mindless monsters. The surrounding water and unscalable edifice defends the Stronghold from vampire attack.

This is close to the height of the Sarafan crusade, and the Stronghold is in its glory.
