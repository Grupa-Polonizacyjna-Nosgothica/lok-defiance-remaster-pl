The most senior and powerful of Nosgoth's surviving vampires, Vorador has witnessed the near-extinction of his race play out at the hands of Moebius's mercenary army of vampire hunters. He has seen his kind oppressed and persecuted throughout history, and has come to regard humans as little more than brutish cattle.

In the era of the Sarafan crusade, Vorador took his revenge upon the Circle for their sponsorship of the vampire purge, and for the murder of his sire, the infamous ancient Vampire Janos Audron. After slaughtering six of the Guardians, he retreated from the world in disillusionment, and forever renounced the affairs of mankind.

Nearly five centuries later, however, Kain's ill-fated assassination of William the Just reignited the humans' genocidal hatred of vampires. This reckless act ultimately led to the annihilation of the vampire race, and Vorador was eventually captured and executed by Moebius's hunters.

Raziel discovers that Vorador was not only the first human in Nosgoth to receive the dark gift from the ancient Vampires, but also the legendary swordsmith responsible for forging the Reaver blade destined to become his prison.

Resolving to find the vampire patriarch in search of answers, Raziel seeks out his majestic domain, nestled deep in the dense swamps of the Termogent Forest. But this is the era of Moebius's genocide - it is but a matter of time before Vorador's sanctuary will be broached, and he, like all his kindred, must succumb to the fate prescribed them...
