EARLY CITADEL - PASSAGEWAY

The only brand new location to be featured in Legacy of Kain: Defiance, the Vampire Citadel (or Guardian Citadel) was extensively iterated on over a slew of concept artworks, with many potential alternative visions set aside.

These early interior rooms - created by lead artist Jacob Wendler - are largely representative of its final, Gaudi-influenced design, but showcase some noteworthy distinctions. They feature more subdued, moody lighting than the finished article, and red motifs are prevalent - more overtly representative of the Vampires' curse.
