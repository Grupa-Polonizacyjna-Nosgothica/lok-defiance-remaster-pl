DESIGNER COMMENTARY #2
Chapter House - Raziel

"The end of SR2 saw Raziel and Kain ending their journey in the Stronghold Chapter house. This chapter starts as Raziel shifts into Spectral and the world warps around him...

The Chapter house is a two level area, the 2nd floor, a balcony is accessed via stairs from the ground floor, Sluagh cavort on the balcony. The ground floor exit Kain used is an impassible solid wall in Spectral, but upstairs a new gateway has appeared that leads to the Chain Gallery and can be phased through by Raziel to progress."
