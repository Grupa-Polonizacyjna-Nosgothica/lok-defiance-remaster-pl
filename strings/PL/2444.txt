The shapes and forms of all physical matter were once presided over by the original Vampire Guardian of States, who now rests within a tomb within the Vampire Citadel.

Quiescent Transformation: This spell turns the Guardian into mist, making her invulnerable to attack. In this form, she has a dash attack which envelops and holds her target in place if it lands. Right before the dash hits, the Guardian will shift back into a solid form for a moment. This gives one the chance to bat the attack away, if time runs out to dodge it.

Vapor Ball: This is a basic projectile attack, similar to those of all her fellow Guardians.
