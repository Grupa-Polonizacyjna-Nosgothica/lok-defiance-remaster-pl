History abhors a paradox.

Fatalism is a norm within Nosgoth - every creature is subject to the controlling hand of destiny. The time-stream follows a conservation of reality which is near-impossible to divert. Under ordinary circumstances, it is impossible.

All souls are bound to one predestined path - and free will, in effect, is an illusion. Drop a stone into a rushing river - the current simply courses around it and flows on as if the obstruction were never there.

Should one travel back in time, they will, inevitably, find that their actions in the past cannot alter history - for they always traveled back in time, and were compelled to.

The continuum of history in this world is, fundamentally, too strong - too resilient - to be derailed.
