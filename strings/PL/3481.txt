Kain returns to the Sarafan Stronghold, the stringently-defended sanctuary of an order of crusaders dedicated to the annihilation of his kind.

Here, he pursues the prophetic sorcerer in command of the brotherhood, Moebius the Time Streamer - his nemesis across the ages, and the one being who can reveal to him the whereabouts of Raziel, his former lieutenant who holds the key to his destiny.

Somewhere within this reputedly impenetrable monastic fortress, this devious manipulator awaits. But although the wretched Sarafan themselves pose him no great threat, Kain knows too well that Moebius, if anyone, will have meticulously prepared for his infiltration...
