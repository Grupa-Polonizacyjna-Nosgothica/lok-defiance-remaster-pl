This Demon variant has seemingly been spawned from the lightning-pummelled depths of a wind-blown and storm-enraged alternate dimension. This Demon usually prefers close-range attacks, but can easily supplement itself at long range with its quick and accurate lightning projectiles.

Crack of Lightning: This bread-and-butter attack utilizes the Lightning Demon's massive pincers to call out and focus the tremendous electrical forces that writhe within the confines of its infernal body. These blasts are as quick and deadly as a lightning bolt from the sky.

Electrical Displacement: The secondary ability of the Lightning Demon is its capacity to teleport itself between two points. As quick as a bolt from the blue, the Lightning Demon is able to dissolve its physical form into pure electricity. Thus transformed, the Lightning Demon can instantaneously travel through the Material Realm and reform its body at a completely different location. This ability is extremely versatile, for it provides both a quick escape in times of trouble, and serves as a devious and offensive strategic move.
