At the moment of their banishment, the Hylden retaliated by unleashing a counter-curse upon the Vampires. The affliction which befell the Vampires transformed their race into parasitical blood-drinkers - the irony being their degradation from preservers of life to predators.

The Ancients were now "vampires" in the traditional sense - preying upon primitive humans for sustenance, and recoiling from the life-giving light. Turned into immortal creatures of darkness, vulnerable to the natural elements they had revered, they agonized in their pathos.

But the Hylden, too, would begin to be deformed. Swept away from the Material Realm and sealed in this strange plane of existence, they found that the influence of this hellish place warped their appearances - devolving them into emaciated parodies of their former beauty.
