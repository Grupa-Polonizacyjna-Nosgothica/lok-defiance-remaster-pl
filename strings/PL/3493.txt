Kain - to his own great surprise - awakes in the Demon Realm, having miraculously survived the loss of the Heart of Darkness.

Beset by hosts of demons as he endures the taunts of their Hylden masters, he battles his way through this maddening hellscape, compelled by an unknown force to return to the Vampire Citadel. By now, Janos has surely been resurrected, and taken prisoner by the Hylden, who will wreak their vengeance.

What of Raziel, the Pillars, and the Reaver? Has Kain misread the signs - missed his cast at destiny - or is the coin still turning for Nosgoth?
