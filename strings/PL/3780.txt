THE WATER FORGE

One of the earliest locations to be designed for Defiance – and the largest original area to be entirely cut – this incarnation of the Water Forge was intended to maintain continuity with the Reaver Forges of Soul Reaver 2 (2001).

When this version of the Forge was conceived, only the Earth and Spirit Forges were also slated for inclusion, with the game scenario omitting the four Forges Raziel had already solved in Soul Reaver 2. Raziel would visit this Forge after exiting the Cemetery, and would proceed to the Pillars after imbuing the Reaver.

This large complex consists of three major sections: the approach to the area, the Forge exterior, and the interior. Raziel was required to manipulate water channels using the Reavers he had already obtained in order to progress to the next level.

Apparently, no more than the first floor of the interior was designed and built before production ceased on the area, and the Forges underwent alterations as a whole.

In its final iteration, the game incorporated all seven Reaver Forges at the Vampire Citadel, with the Water Forge being accessed via a teleporter in Vorador's Mansion. Virtually no evidence of the original Water Forge remained in the game.
