Introduced: Blood Omen: Legacy of Kain (1996)

The antique burial ground of a forgotten race, this sprawling Cemetery has long since been claimed by humankind, and even now vampire hunters freely congregate here, daring to tread upon graves and disturb the peace in hopes of finding prey lurking among the tombstones.

Generations of Nosgoth's dead repose here, corpses layered and compounded atop one another. The most ancient architecture, subtle and obscured amidst the mausoleums erected by humans, is apparently dedicated to the worship of darkness and light.

Mass graves, crypts and tombs of servants, nobles and kings alike are present in this place, all equals together now in death.
