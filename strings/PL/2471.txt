The most basic and common form of Hylden possession is that of a human corpse. Boasting the least strength, agility and endurance, these are the weakest form of Revenant. Due to the proliferation of human corpses across Nosgoth in the current era, they are also the most common.

Grave Spit: The ranged ability of the Human Revenant is a projectile of green Hylden energy, which is literally spat at the victim. This projectile does not track, and deals minimal damage. The Human Revenant does not rely heavily upon it, but uses it infrequently when its prey is out of melee range.
