Both Kain and Raziel employ Telekinetic force to hurl enemies away or shatter obstacles.

Initially, Kain's TK powers are much more developed than Raziel's. From the beginning, Kain can swiftly blast enemies away, or lift them into the air and hurl them in any direction. Early on, he acquires the ability to ignite or douse certain flammable objects using TK.

Raziel's telekinetic powers are much weaker than Kain's at first. Though he can fire quick TK bursts to hurl enemies away or shatter obstacles, he cannot lift and throw enemies until later on.
