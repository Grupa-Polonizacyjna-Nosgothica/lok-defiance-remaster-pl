The divergent philosophies of the Vampires and the Hylden fell into direct opposition, and, ultimately, these differences in method and intention tore them apart. Similarly matched in power, they turned their respective magical arts against one another.

A series of great wars flamed between the Elder Races for a thousand years, and would come to destroy both races, victor and vanquished alike. The conflict between them was of almost apocalyptic proportions.

As the onslaught escalated, the Hylden neared completion of their ultimate weapon - the Device, with which they could threaten to loose destruction upon the land, and prevail against the Vampires. Only one component was necessary for them to complete the Device - a Vampire, whose blood and life energy would power the machine.
