VORADOR'S MANSION - FUTURE

Early in production, a visit to Nosgoth's "future" era (a century after the fall of both the Pillars and the Mansion) was slated - the same time period to which Raziel was shunted by Moebius in Soul Reaver 2 (2001).

This detour was intended to reconcile Vorador's inexplicable appearance in Blood Omen 2 (2002), long after his death, with the series' continuity - and entailed his resurrection at the hands of either Raziel or Kain, in a subplot already planned but cut once before from Soul Reaver 2.


LA VITA NUOVA

This room, created by artist Jennifer Fernández, was intended to serve as the repose of Vorador's headless body - having been recovered and zealously guarded by an ascetic human cult of vampire worshipers.

The cultists were to be led by a younger Umah, Vorador's closest vampire lieutenant from Blood Omen 2. Kain (in Defiance's iteration of the quest) was charged with recovering Vorador's head from the Sarafan Stronghold, and returning to reunite it with its owner. He would witness Vorador rise, and transform Umah into a vampire - thus begetting Blood Omen 2's vampire race.
