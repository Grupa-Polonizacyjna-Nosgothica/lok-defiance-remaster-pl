This is the larger of the two varieties of statues that Kain and Raziel encounter. Once activated, the shoulders, eyes and head of the statue flame with virulent ferocity. Their attacks are strong and swift, causing any unaware victim to be thrown upon contact. The Guardian Construct has not only been blessed with massive strength and endurance, but also a magical flaming shield which protects them from harm.

Fiery Shield: This shield protects the Guardian Construct, coating their stone skin in wreaths of blazing fire. As long as this shield engulfs the Construct, any attacks levied against it will cause a backlash of flame that will sear and incinerate the attacker.

Fireball: The Construct will pause with its sword held upright as it concentrates and focuses its fiery energy into a projectile.
