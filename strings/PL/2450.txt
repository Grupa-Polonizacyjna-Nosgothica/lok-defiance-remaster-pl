Janos is the last surviving member of the ancient original Vampire race, bearing the blue skin and black wings of his long-dead brethren. Upon the collapse of the Pillars, he becomes possessed by the Hylden Lord and turns on Raziel. While possessed, Janos is practically invulnerable to attack; however, after expending energy, the spirit possessing him can be momentarily fought back by Janos, rendering him vulnerable - if just for a moment - to further assault.

Air Jump: This is his most common action, linking three different attacks: Hawk Dive, Zephyr's Wind and Torrent of Air. While he is in the air, there will be a brief moment to observe and detect which attack is coming.

Hawk Dive: During this attack, Janos will swoop down at his victim with frightening speed. He will aim in on their ground position, so one must dodge backward at the last second to avoid this attack. Once Janos hits the ground, he will be vulnerable for a moment or two.

Zephyr's Wind: Janos will remain hovering in the air after completing the Air Jump. Using his broad, fiery wings, he will emit a forward-facing orange cyclone that can only be dodged by quickly moving left or right.

Torrent of Wind: Also hovering in the air after completing the Air Jump, Janos will then begin to charge a massive magical attack which takes several seconds to complete. This eruption is effectively impossible to dodge.

Call of Possession: The Hylden Lord can use Janos to facilitate the possession of nearby corpses with Hylden souls. When triggered, this magic will cause several Revenants to emerge from the ground and fight by his side.

Cyclone Spin: This is one of his only ground-based attacks. He will spin in a small circle with his wings outstretched and knock those in proximity back if too close.

TK Push: Like all elder vampires, Janos possesses innate telekinetic powers, and will often force his enemies away if they come too close.
