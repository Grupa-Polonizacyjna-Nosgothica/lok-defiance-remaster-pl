The Wheel of Fate is the doctrine of the Elder God: the inexorable cycle of birth and death and rebirth.

As souls pass into the Spectral Realm, and are inevitably devoured therein by the Elder's agents, they are born anew - supposedly purified - as the Wheel turns.

To the Elder, vampires are an abomination: their souls remain stagnant, trapped within their immortal bodies. The plague of their kind - in the view of the Elder and its minions, at least - obstructs the flow of life and death, and leeches the land of its spirtual strength.

Raziel, despite previously serving the Elder, has become convinced that it represents a far greater parasitic force than the vampire race - thriving in the prevalence of madness and misery - and that the Wheel represents an endless, cycling trap. He has little option but to churn it, however; as a devourer of souls, he, too, sustains the Elder with each and every spirit he consumes.
