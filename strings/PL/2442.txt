Once governing the plants, animals and all forms of natural life in Nosgoth, this specter served as the original Vampire Guardian of Nature.

Nature's Grip: The Guardian will call forth several Entangling Roots to hold their victims in their grip.

Fury of Terra: Multiple glowing projectiles can be unleashed, and they will linger around and continue to home in on their foes. These are very slow compared to other Guardians' projectiles, but when combined with the Tentacles, they can quickly complicate one's fighting area.
