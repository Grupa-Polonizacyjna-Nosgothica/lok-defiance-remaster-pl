Introduced: Legacy of Kain: Soul Reaver (1999)

The Elder God's origins are shrouded in mystery, and few in Nosgoth even know of its dark existence. Its tentacles coil throughout the deep and remote places of Nosgoth's murky Underworld, and its subtle influence reaches across the length and breadth of the land.

It was the Elder God who saved Raziel from the Abyss, resurrected him as a devourer of souls, and unleashed him upon Nosgoth to wreak his revenge. The Elder God urged its newly-fledged "soul reaver" to reap the vampires' unholy souls and end Kain's death-grip on the decaying land.

Dominating the Spectral Realm, the Elder is sustained by the souls devoured by its predatorial agents, and describes itself as the hub of the Wheel of Fate - Nosgoth's preternatural cycle of birth, death, and rebirth. As Raziel consumes souls, their energy is siphoned to sustain the Elder God.

Though Raziel fulfilled part of his dark covenant with the Elder, destroying several of his closest brethren, he increasingly came to distrust his master's motivations. Regarding it and its Wheel of Fate as a parasitic force - condemning Nosgoth to spiritual stagnancy - he defied its will to destroy Kain, and thus incurred its wrath.

Kneeling in its presence once again, Raziel is confronted with the revelation that the Elder God is not some minor demigod to be trifled with and mocked, but is truly a vast and omnipresent entity whose influence spans all Nosgoth.

It is made abundantly clear that the Elder God is no longer Raziel's benefactor. Raziel realizes that his relative safety in the spirit world, and the abundance of planar portals, were advantages granted of the Elder God's beneficence. Without the Elder God's help, Raziel is a virtual prisoner in the spirit world. He must find a way to escape the snares of his destiny, and exert his own free will.
