Introduced: Blood Omen: Legacy of Kain (1996)

A militaristic leader among his race, the Hylden Lord is the same dark entity responsible for possessing the body of Mortanius, murdering Ariel, and orchestrating the corruption of the Pillars. Despite his extremist methods, he regards himself as a champion of justice and freedom, his agenda revolving entirely around the Pillars' destruction.

Capable of possessing beings within the material world from within their otherworldly prison, the Hylden Lord and his followers seek to unravel the Binding, and thus secure their freedom. To this end, they have continually sought to influence and railroad Raziel, who has crucially delivered the key to their victory by resurrecting Janos Audron.

With the Binding loosened by a younger Kain's refusal at the Pillars, the Hylden Lord has seized the opportunity to possess Janos. Using Janos's powers, he will draw fellow Hylden through to the Material Realm, laying the foundations for a full-scale invasion.

In the future, in the guise of the Sarafan Lord, he will raise an army of neo-Sarafan to battle the increasing "vampire menace" - managing to nearly destroy a younger Kain, and steal the Soul Reaver from him, thus leaking further Hylden influence into the world.

Vengeful, destructive, and scarred both physically and mentally by aeons of imprisonment within the Demon Realm, the Hylden Lord will stop at nothing to pave the foundations for both liberation and revenge in his enemies' blood.
