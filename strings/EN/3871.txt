THE MACHINERY ROOM

Containing a large mechanism of unknown purpose, this geometric room - built by lead artist Jacob Wendler - bears the characteristic hallmarks of Hylden architecture.

Its stylings and contents are particularly reminiscent of Legacy of Kain: The Dark Prophecy's take on Malek's Bastion, a level from Blood Omen: Legacy of Kain (1996) reinterpreted by Ritual Entertainment - which itself featured mechanisms used to birth Sarafan wraiths.

The commonalities imply, perhaps, that this room served as a basis or source of inspiration for the latter.
