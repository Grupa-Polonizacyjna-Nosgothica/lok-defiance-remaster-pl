Counseled by Vorador to seek the Heart of Darkness in Avernus, Raziel is driven by an even greater urgency than before. The city is in flames, assaulted by a plague of demons summoned by the deranged sorceress, Azimuth, from other dimensions.

Raziel must make haste to discover the Heart and restore Janos Audron to life, before both are lost forever. In Avernus, he will uncover long-forgotten mysteries of past and future alike, and confront one of Nosgoth's darkest enemies face-to-face.

Perhaps, at last, Raziel stands on the precipice of enlightenment. Yet the ancient prophecies claiming only one hero will survive loom large...
