THE COMBAT ARENA - KAIN

Players of Soul Reaver 2 (2001) may recognize this as a version of that game's introductory area - the Circle of Nine's Gathering Room, deep within the Sarafan Stronghold.

Following the precedent set by Vorador, we have chosen to repurpose this octagonal space as a combat arena (disabling your invincibility in the process).

Have fun sparring with a bevy of enemies from across the game - or hang back, lurk and observe from the periphery as they do battle with each other.
