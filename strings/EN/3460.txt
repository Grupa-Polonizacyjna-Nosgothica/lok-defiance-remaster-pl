Introduced: Legacy of Kain: Defiance (2003)

Long ago defiled and abandoned, this fortress of the ancient Vampire race - also referred to as the Guardian Citadel - was the stronghold of the original Circle of Nine, the oligarchy of sorcerer-guardians who were summoned to represent the Pillars.

The Citadel stands upon an island in the Lake of Tears, littered with the ruins of the Ancients' civilization. Six elemental forges remain intact, which contain a sequence of murals recording the history of the Vampires, and the tombs of the original Guardians.

At the heart of the Citadel lies a seventh prominent chamber - the contents of which are enigmatic.
