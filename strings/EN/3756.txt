THE EARLY UNDERWORLD

This alternative take on the Underworld area precedes the overarching redesign which also overhauled the Sarafan Stronghold. Initially, Raziel's story began in the Stronghold like Kain's, and the Underworld was first conceived as a shorter climax to the beginning of his story, as opposed to its own full-fledged game region and tutorial.

Falling from the Pool Room of the Stronghold into this shadowy cavern, Raziel would interact with the wrathful Elder God, before fleeing through a tunnel formed by the intertwined mass of its writhing limbs. This long passage facilitated his journey to the Cemetery, similar to his progression in the finished game.

Sharing some commonalities with the final version of the Underworld, such as the central platform, the Underworld here nevertheless adheres to a much darker vision than its counterpart - more explicitly emphasizing the Lovecraftian, "cosmic horror" stylings of the Elder God and his domain.
