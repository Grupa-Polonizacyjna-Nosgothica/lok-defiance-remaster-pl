As the vampire population grew, and many fledglings hunted indiscriminately, the human Circle took action to stem the vampiric tide. They instated the order of the Sarafan - a monastic sect of warrior-priests - to mercilessly exterminate the vampire clans. The Sarafan's genocidal crusade - known as the Vampire Purge - lasted over a century and resulted in the near-extinction of Nosgoth's vampire races.

At the height of the purge - the same era in which Kain's journey begins - Janos Audron was hunted down and killed by the Sarafan, who tore his living heart out of his body. His demise brought about the extinction of the ancient Vampire race. The Sarafan secreted away their stolen trophy, and christened it the Heart of Darkness.

Raziel believes that only Janos can unravel the mystery of his fate. Willful as ever, he is determined to undo Janos's death, and thus seeks to reclaim the Heart of Darkness - despite Kain's frantic efforts to dissuade him.
