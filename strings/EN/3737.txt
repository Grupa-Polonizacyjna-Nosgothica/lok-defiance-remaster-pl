CATHEDRAL PINNACLE

Our penultimate offering comes not from the Defiance production period, but from that of Legacy of Kain: Soul Reaver (1999). This is the final block in the puzzle previously illuminated by the Lost Levels of Soul Reaver I & II Remastered (2024) - the Pinnacle of the Silenced Cathedral.

This room served as a connector between Zephon's boss room and the Cathedral exterior leading to Kain's Mountain Retreat, as well as the staging for the final moments of the game. Here, after destroying Kain, Raziel would unblock the Cathedral's sounding pipes to unleash a hymn of death across Nosgoth, destroying all the vampires in the land.
