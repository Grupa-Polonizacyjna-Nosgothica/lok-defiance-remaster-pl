THE SANCTUARY OF THE CLANS

First appearing in Legacy of Kain: Soul Reaver (1999), the Sanctuary of the Clans is the seat of Kain's empire, a grand monument built to encompass the broken Pillars in the centuries beyond Legacy of Kain: Defiance.

Though briefly recalled in archive footage during Defiance's introduction cinematic, the Sanctuary was originally set to appear as a playable location as well. Here, we present the fruits of original Defiance environment artist Isabelle Lemay's efforts to reconstruct the Sanctuary on newer hardware, with some additions by Patrick Johnson.

A MONUMENT TO AMBITION

Chronologically positioned some 2,000 years after the Pillars' collapse, the Soul Reaver era and the Sanctuary it housed acted at the setpiece for what was originally the final chapter of Defiance. This epilogue was culled so early in production that no dialogue was ever scripted to support it.

This version of the Sanctuary is not an exact replica of the Legacy of Kain: Soul Reaver original, featuring many more columns and chairs at the periphery of the Pillars. It shares a number of architectural commonalities with the Water Forge, which, in turn, were ultimately transferred to the Vampire Citadel in the final game.
ANOTHER FATEFUL CONFRONTATION?

Kain awaits you on the central dais, poised to recreate his epic battle with you from the original Legacy of Kain: Soul Reaver. Don't fret - on this occasion, your former master is in the mood for more jocular duelling, his deadly lightning bolt attacks and other powers held in reserve.

Very early designs of Soul Reaver 2 (2001) similarly prescribed a final confrontation between Raziel and Kain at the Sanctuary of the Clans - an epic philosophical clash played out at the conclusion of Raziel's quest.
