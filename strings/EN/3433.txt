Five centuries apart from Kain, Raziel too explores the surroundings of the Pillars - another remnant of the long-dead ancient Vampires' civilization, whose ruins he continues to unearth.

After learning of Vorador's role in forging the Reaver, Raziel seeks a rendezvous - or a confrontation - with the elder vampire to unravel the mysteries of his fate, but must first visit Ariel, the specter of the Pillars, to obtain directions to his refuge.

He is impeded by Moebius's vampire-hunting mobs, assembled to annihilate the race he once belonged to, and must not tarry in his endeavor, lest they themselves reach Vorador first.
