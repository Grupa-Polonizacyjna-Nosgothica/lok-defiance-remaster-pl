THE DARK PROPHECY DEMO

Legacy of Kain: The Dark Prophecy, the cancelled sequel to Defiance, entered development under Ritual Entertainment in 2004, but its production was discontinued later the same year.

Built on the same engine as Defiance, The Dark Prophecy returned Kain to the role of singular protagonist, and pursued a storyline heavily focused around the Hylden, the Demon Realm, and the fate of Janos Audron.

A number of locations were designed and developed prior to the game's cancellation, and this demo showcases one such playable sequence - a "vertical slice" of the game.
MOUNTAINS OF MADNESS

Emerging in a grisly place laden with bloodied corpses and the evidence of dark rituals, Kain must traverse the mountain pass, battling a host of hostile forces in the process.

It is believed that the unnamed area observed in the demo was to be explored in the game's opening stages. It serves as a transitional location bridging more major landmarks, and offers a representative insight into the project's gameplay.

Enjoy exploring this time capsule, a remnant of what might have been, and clashing with exclusive enemy classes imported from the original demo.
