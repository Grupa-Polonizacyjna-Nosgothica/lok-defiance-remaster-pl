When the Pillars of Nosgoth were created, a timeless process was set into motion. Each of the nine Pillars selected a Guardian to represent and protect it, and, as long as these nine Guardians remained "pure" and loyal to their duty, the Binding remained secure.

The first Guardians responsible for summoning the Pillars were nine of the original Vampires. As long as these Guardians lived, their duty was to serve the Pillar they represented - if a Guardian died, the Pillar would choose another, born with the destiny to serve that Pillar.

The Pillars were crucial to sustaining the banishment of the Hylden - and the Pillar Guardians ensured the health and strength of the Pillars. As long as the Pillars remained uncorrupted, the Binding was secure; if the Pillars were weakened, the Binding was loosened.
