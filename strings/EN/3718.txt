THE COLLAPSED PILLARS

This apocalyptic scene represents the immediate aftermath of Kain's fateful decision to refuse to sacrifice his own life and restore the Pillars, thus condemning the Land of Nosgoth to millennia of ruin and decay.

The Pillars lie in a myriad of scattered shards, their supernatural cores exposed. The clearing stands sundered beneath an ashen sky, marred by the infernal energy blast unleashed with the unravelling of the Binding.

This area provided the staging for a scene cut from the story script in the earliest redrafting stage. After escaping the Demon Realm, Kain would emerge in the Material Realm within walking distance of the clearing, where he would witness his younger self and Ariel from afar, debating the ethics of his choice.

While the young Kain delivered his justifications – uncannily reminiscent of Raziel's rhetoric against Ariel in Soul Reaver 2 (2001) - the despondent and desperate specter would flail at him, willing his death, but to no avail as he departed.

This Pillars layout differs somewhat from those featured in the final game, containing multiple flooded vistas which impede further exploration. No connectors to adjacent areas exist, prompting Kain to use bat flight to proceed to the story's concluding sequences at the Vampire Citadel.
