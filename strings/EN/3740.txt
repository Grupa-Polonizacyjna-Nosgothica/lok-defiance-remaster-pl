THE EARLY UNDERWORLD

This alternative take on the Underworld area precedes the overarching redesign which also overhauled the Stronghold. Initially, Raziel's story began in the Stronghold like Kain's, and the Underworld was first conceived as a shorter climax to the beginning of his story, as opposed to its own full-fledged game region and tutorial.

Falling from the Stronghold's Pool Room into this shadowy cavern, Raziel would interact with the wrathful Elder God, before fleeing through a tunnel formed by the intertwined mass of its writhing limbs. This passage facilitated his journey to the Cemetery, as in the finished game.
THE STYGIAN DEPTHS

Sharing some commonalities with the final version of the Underworld, such as the central platform, the Underworld here nevertheless adheres to a much darker vision than its counterpart - more explicitly emphasizing the Lovecraftian, "cosmic horror" stylings of the Elder God and his domain.

These atmospheric choices harken back to the Underworld as portrayed in early versions of Legacy of Kain: Soul Reaver (1999) - before that, too, was relit, prefiguring the future by adopting a palette and brightness scheme more similar to that of Defiance's final version.
