Though the Pillars continue to keep the Hylden locked away in the Demon Realm, the Binding also effectively ensures their immortality. Meanwhile, the Circle of Nine, now co-opted by humanity, would launch genocidal crusades against Nosgoth's vampires - ironic, given they were the Guardians of a monument created by their supposed enemies.

The Scion of Balance is the messianic figure prophesied by the ancient Vampires, who is destined to return the Pillars to vampiric rule. Kain has reason to believe that he himself is this legendary hero - that he was ordained to restore the Pillars to their rightful inheritors, and that his intended destiny has been railroaded by Moebius.

Janos, on the other hand, interpreted Raziel as the champion of his race - vaguely represented in prophecies as a blue-skinned, idealized winged hero with eyes of fire.

If these long-forgotten premonitions hold truth, who is truly destined to redeem Nosgoth - and by what means?
