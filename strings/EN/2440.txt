Centuries ago, this specter served in life as the Vampire Guardian of Energy, who ruled over the wild arching power that spawns the storm and sparks life itself.

Sky Fury: This spell is his only form of direct attack, calling lightning from his fingertips to blast in his enemy's direction. This is a difficult projectile to dodge due to its speed. Fortunately, it takes a long time to cast.

Tempest's Cloak: Using his control over the omnipresent surges of energy, this Guardian can imbue his own body or the bodies of his allies with a shocking force. Any physical attack upon the affected target, while in this state, will reflect just as much damage back upon the attacker.
