Full-fledged Cenobites have the ability to summon Fire Demons to fight for them in combat. They can cast a protective glyph shield around themselves, whose floating runes will absorb all damage until they are destroyed. They can also serve a support role in combat by projecting elemental fire from the Demon Realm at their enemies.

Nether Rift: The first and most powerful of their abilities is a ritual to call servants from the Demon Realm. This servant takes the form of the formidable Fire Demon. Once summoned, this creature will be forever loyal to the Cenobites, even if the original Summoner dies.

Demonic Benedictions: Their second set of abilities consists of rituals to bless their kindred in faith and summoned Demons with unholy blessings. These blessings can take many forms.
