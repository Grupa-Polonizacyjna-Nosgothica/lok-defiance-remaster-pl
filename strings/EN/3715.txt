THE HALL OF FAME

This small museum-like hall - codenamed "mdlview1", but affectionately dubbed the "Hall of Fame" by Defiance Remastered tools and UI programmer Andrew Fradley - was originally a test room created by Defiance's art director, Daniel Cabuco, to preview 3D models.

The textures seen here are largely reused from prior Legacy of Kain games, with the stained glass windows around the room perhaps familiar to players of Soul Reaver Remastered - these are the same window designs found at the heart of Kain's Mountain Retreat, another Lost Level from that game.

The Defiance Remastered team found it an appropriate place to safekeep the various statues of the ancient Vampires. Feel free to examine their models in greater detail.
