A religious autocracy, Avernus is the largest city in Nosgoth, presided over by its Matriarch, Azimuth the Planer - the sorceress representing the Pillar of Dimension. Avernus has been built around the central structure that dominates the center of town - the massive Avernus Cathedral.

In times previous, Avernus thrived, but during this period, the deranged Azimuth has unleashed a plague of demons upon the city, half-decimated by fire. Beneath the pristine exterior of the Cathedral run the warrens of the Catacombs, macabre dens of dark arts and blood sacrifices.

The hypocritically serene Cathedral above belies the dichotomous core of corruption within.
