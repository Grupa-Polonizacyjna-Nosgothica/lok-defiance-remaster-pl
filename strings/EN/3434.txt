With the Vampire Citadel now revealed to him, Kain begins his exploration of the long-abandoned fortress of his ancient ancestors, where his answers supposedly lie written in stone.

Moebius claims Kain will find evidence here that Raziel is not the savior figure he envisages - that he will inevitably destroy Kain. It is, in truth, not Raziel's destiny, but Kain's own role as Scion of Balance that he has come here to uncover - but will both prove inextricably intertwined?

And what was it that drove the ancient Vampires to extinction, aeons ago? Kain must tread carefully as he investigates these mysteries.
