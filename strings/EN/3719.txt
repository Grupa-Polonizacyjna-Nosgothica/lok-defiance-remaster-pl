THE EARLY FORGE

The purpose and provenance of this mysterious test room is unknown, save that it was another of those created by Defiance environment artist Isabelle Lemay. Its textures are unique, largely dissimilar to any observed either in the retail version of Defiance, or in previous Legacy of Kain games.

The hall's most prominent feature is the large sundial occupying the lower tier, which is derived from the Dark Forge of Soul Reaver 2 (2001). In recognition of this minimal clue, the room has been tentatively dubbed the Early Forge by the Defiance Remastered team.
