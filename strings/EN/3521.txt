The Hylden race stands all but forgotten, both within and without the Circle - even the mere recitation of their name becoming forbidden taboo among the Guardians. Though still banished by the Pillars' sorcery, they have devised other means to influence events within Nosgoth.

As the Pillars decay, the Binding weakens - the membrane between the dimensions grows thin, enabling some Hylden insurgents to possess the moldering flesh of corpses. They may enter the physical world in this form, rising from the grave to assail Kain and Raziel.

The Hylden have also, to various degrees, obtained the ability to possess living hosts and subvert them to their cause. Typically, this involves the invasion of the host by a transient disembodied Hylden soul. Once a vessel is successfully overtaken, the effects can be varied.

Lighter possessions merely increase the strength and ferocity of the victim, while the heaviest can not only repurpose the mind for their uses, but the very skin and bone as well. In these heaviest of cases, the body of the host is twisted and disfigured into monstrous size and deformity.

The tendrils of the Hylden's influence permeate the land, not least when sorcerers unwisely choose to commune with them. They wish only that their ancient enemies (and their enemies' descendants) be annihilated, and that the Binding be destroyed, in order to escape exile and reclaim Nosgoth.
