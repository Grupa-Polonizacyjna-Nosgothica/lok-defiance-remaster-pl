AVERNUS - HELL WALKWAY

Although the Avernus Catacombs comprise little more than a sequence of basic hallways in the final product, a grander, more diverse vision was, inevitably, conceived at first.

These subterranean hallways, tunnels and balconies - the work of artists Stephane Gaudette and Jeff Johnsrude - are more reminiscent of the Catacombs' Blood Omen: Legacy of Kain (1996) incarnation both in layout and aesthetic, and better emphasize their "fire and brimstone" conceits.
