Free will is an illusion in the Land of Nosgoth - the hand of fate steers Kain and Raziel in their explorations. The Foresight ability represents the unseen pull guiding them towards their destined paths.

Foresight is used to highlight points of the correct route toward an objective. It is especially useful in large areas, multi-level chambers, or any location where the path may branch or loop.
