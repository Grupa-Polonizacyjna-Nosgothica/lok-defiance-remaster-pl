Nosgoth's present-day vampire population is derived from former humans. All those turned from the light have received the vampiric curse from a siring vampire, which relates them to the bloodline of the ancient Vampires.

These contemporary generations of vampires have come to embrace and revel in the predatory blood-thirst that drives them - regarding it not as the Blood Curse, but as the Dark Gift, a power whose temptations must be indulged.

Physically stronger than humans, fledgling vampires can be distinguished by their characteristic fanged teeth, pointed ears, and golden eyes. As they evolve, adult and elder vampires develop cloven hands and feet in the likeness of the Ancients, but adopt an increasingly monstrous visage.

Vampires are universally vulnerable to the touch of water and the light of the sun. The Dark Gift manifests differently in each vampire, granting every individual unique traits.
