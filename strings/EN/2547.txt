(200 years after Blood Omen: Legacy of Kain)

· Following the collapse of the Pillars, Kain becomes a commander of an army of vampires bent on ruling the land of Nosgoth.
In response to his rise to power, another faction appears in the land: the Neo-Sarafan, a group of militant humans led by a mysterious and powerful figure, the Sarafan Lord.

· Determined to wipe out the vampire threat, the Sarafan clash with Kain's army for years.
This war ends with the Sarafan finally triumphant over Kain's army, and with Kain and the Sarafan Lord locked in mortal combat.
The Sarafan Lord wins this battle, strips the Soul Reaver from Kain, and throws him off a cliff to his apparent death.

· Kain spends the next 200 years in a hibernation-like state, recovering from his near-fatal wounds.

(400 years after Blood Omen: Legacy of Kain)

· Kain awakens in a strange city with almost no memory of his former self. Another vampire, Umah, has taken him in and revived him to health.

· Umah tells Kain that his body was found and revived, and he has been brought to the capital of Nosgoth, the city of Meridian. The Sarafan have taken fascist control of most of Nosgoth. Vampires have formed a small resistance, called the Cabal, in Meridian to undermine the Sarafan. It is this resistance that Kain is now a part of.
The goal of the resistance is simple: kill the Sarafan Lord, and the Sarafan will crumble. Because the Sarafan do not know of Kain's existence, he becomes the perfect agent to find and kill the Sarafan Lord.
Kain agrees to help the Cabal for the time being, but he fully intends to follow his own agenda, and seek revenge however possible...

· The events of Blood Omen 2 ensue.
