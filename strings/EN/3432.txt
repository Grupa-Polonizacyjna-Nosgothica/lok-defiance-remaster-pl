Bereft of alternative options in his search for Raziel, Kain reluctantly decides to act on Moebius's dubious advice, and returns to the Pillars of Nosgoth - the great edifices around which his life and unlife have revolved.

In this era, the Pillars stand pure, uncorrupted by the dark forces Kain strives to thwart. As he continues to outmaneuver the Sarafan, Kain contemplates his grander designs - to one day restore the future Pillars to this pristine form.

Here, Kain must seek a vista to the west, where Moebius claims he will find enlightenment on Raziel's ultimate role in the fate of Nosgoth.
