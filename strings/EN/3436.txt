Continuing his exploration of the labyrinthine Vampire Citadel, Kain has discovered a mural depicting two opposing heroes - one championing the Vampire race, the other the Hylden.

The Vampires' savior wields the Reaver - the very blade Kain himself bears. His opponent, with blazing eyes, brandishes a flaming sword - reminiscent of Raziel and the wraith-blade. And, according to the evidence here, it is the Reaver-bearer who is destined to fall in battle.

What to make of these prophecies? Only by completing the Balance Emblem can Kain gain access to the heart of the Citadel, and expose the answers it may hold.
