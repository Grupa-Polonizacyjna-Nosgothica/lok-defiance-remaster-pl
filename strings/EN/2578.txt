Like Kain and Raziel, the Reaver has an insatiable appetite for its enemies' life-force. With each strike, the Reaver absorbs a little of its victim's energy, and the Reaver Gauge begins to fill. Because the energy in the Reaver Gauge decays over time, Kain and Raziel must maintain an intense assault to build the Reaver to a full charge.

When an enemy is reduced to a vulnerable state, Kain and Raziel may forego drinking the victim's blood or devouring its soul, and instead feed the victim's life energy to the Reaver blade, increasing its power temporarily.
