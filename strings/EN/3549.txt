In the early prehistory of Nosgoth (long before recorded "human" history, and millennia before Kain's birth), two races warred with each other for dominance of Nosgoth - the Vampires and the Hylden.

An almost angelic species, these ancient Vampires (note: capital V for the originals; little v for their descendants) were taller and leaner than humans, and blue-skinned, with cloven hands and feet. Perhaps the most striking of their physical features, however, were their almost iridescent, black feathered wings, which rendered them capable of flight.

The original Vampires adhered to a spiritualistic way of life. They worshiped light, and venerated a universal energy, from which they derived the elemental magic they employed. They gloried in their roles as caretakers of the world and the life within. Culturally, they were individualistic, and more loosely-organized than their rivals.
