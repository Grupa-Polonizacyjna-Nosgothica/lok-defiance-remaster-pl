The Hylden, like the Vampires (before either of those terms had the demonic or blood-sucking connotations they currently have, and before their mutual curses altered their appearances), were once preternaturally beautiful beings. They could be physically distinguished by the bony crests about their heads, their vestigial bat-like wings, and their distinctive stilt-like feet.

Unlike the Vampires, however, the Hylden were purveyors of a technological type of magic - the crux of both races' ideological differences. They used organic life with less regard, considering all lower life forms little more than tools and material to be shaped to their ends. Relatively unified in their agendas and beliefs, they operated in a more hive-like fashion than their factional rivals.
