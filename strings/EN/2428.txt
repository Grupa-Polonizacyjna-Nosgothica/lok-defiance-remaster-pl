The Reaper Archon is the smaller of the two types of Archons. They are built more for speed and quickness than strength, and prefer to attack while floating along the spectral currents they effortlessly swim through.

Draining Leash: The Reaper Archon's primary form of attack, other than swiping with their claw-like forearms, is a leash-like chain of energy, which can be latched onto its prey. As long as the chain of energy is intact, the Reaper Archon will continually drain health from the victim. However, this draining link between hunter and hunted can be disrupted if the Reaper Archon is attacked and successfully hit.
