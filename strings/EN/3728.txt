THE SANCTUARY OF THE CLANS

First appearing in Legacy of Kain: Soul Reaver (1999), the Sanctuary of the Clans is the seat of Kain's empire, a grand monument built to encompass the broken Pillars in the centuries beyond Legacy of Kain: Defiance.

Though briefly recalled in archive footage during Defiance's introduction cinematic, the Sanctuary was originally set to appear as a playable location as well. Here, we present the fruits of original Defiance environment artist Isabelle Lemay's efforts to reconstruct the Sanctuary on newer hardware.

Chronologically positioned some 2,000 years after the Pillars' collapse, the Soul Reaver era and the Sanctuary it housed acted at the setpiece for what was originally the final chapter of Defiance. This epilogue was culled so early in production that no dialogue was ever scripted to support it.

This version of the Sanctuary is not an exact replica of the Legacy of Kain: Soul Reaver original, featuring many more columns and chairs at the periphery of the Pillars. It shares a number of architectural commonalities with the Water Forge, which, in turn, were ultimately transferred to the Vampire Citadel in the final game.
