DESIGNER COMMENTARY #1
Chapter House - Kain

"The end of SR2 saw Raziel and Kain ending their journey in the Stronghold Chapter house. Kain sees (as do we) Raziel shift into Spectral and disappear. Raziel's dead body (Sarafan) lies here... The Chapter starts with a cinematic event where Moebius confronts Kain.

After the cinematic game play resumes. The Chapter house is a two level area, the 2nd floor is a balcony, and is accessed via stairs from the ground floor, but barred by a gate. Kain needs to leave the room, to progress into the stronghold. By using Mist form to move thru the gate and up to the balcony, killing the Magic User to open the Gate. The extraneous doors to SR2 places have portcullis gates across their closed doors."
