The Greater Shade is the largest of the two varieties of Shades. Its dark, turbulent body shifts and swirls with shadows blacker than night. Being the most powerful of its kind, it is as such the most aggressive. Preferring a somewhat close-range stance, this immense ephemeral shadow is a most intimidating opponent.

Shadow Charge: At medium combat range, the Greater Shade can quickly propel itself at its victim, bashing them with their shadow-formed bodies. This move is quick and deadly, but, given it propels the Greater Shade's entire body, it leaves them at a slightly closer range than normally preferred. This, in turn, leaves them slightly more vulnerable to a swift counter-attack.
