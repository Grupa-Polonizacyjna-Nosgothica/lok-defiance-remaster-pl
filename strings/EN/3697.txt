THE DARK FORGE

Another of the Reaver Forges which originally appeared in Soul Reaver 2 (2001), this version of the Dark Forge follows the same cues as the early Air Forge by remixing components of its previous incarnation.

A somewhat more open and vertical layout than its counterpart, it shares little to no commonality with the original Dark Forge in its arrangement, but its set of textures is entirely derivative of that version.

Consisting of three major partitions, the area contains a number of non-functional puzzle setups, which hint at some of the challenges the level designers had planned.

This Forge, like the others, is clearly incomplete, and probably did not advance much further into development before being scrapped in favor of the more boilerplate Forge designs of the final game.
