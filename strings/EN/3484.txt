Having escaped his imprisonment in the Underworld, and defeated the Archons, Raziel surfaces to find himself in an unfamiliar time and place.

Trapped within the eternal twilight of the Spectral Realm, amidst the tombs and mausoleums of an expansive Cemetery, he must improvise a new means of projecting himself into the physical world. Only by thoroughly exploring these ruins, and discovering the ancient secrets herein, can he begin to seek his destiny - or change it.

However, in disturbing those long dead here, he may find that he is not the only being desperate to gain a foothold in the Material Realm...
