The original Vampires had the Reaver forged as a formidable weapon to be used against the Hylden, if and when they managed to return. The Vampires' legends and prophecies foretold of a champion who would arrive to claim the blade and wield it against their enemies.

The Reaver was forged in adherence to prophecy - an undulating blade, over five feet long, with a distinctive hilt, and a fanged skull. With their new magic, the ancient weapon-smiths enchanted the Reaver to be a vampiric weapon, draining the victim's body of its blood.

The Reaver was specially forged as a weapon against the Hylden - it was ensorcelled so that the Vampires' greatest enemy was particularly vulnerable to its attacks.

Kain currently wields this incarnation of the blade, having claimed it when he changed history by preventing it from consuming Raziel's soul - for now.
