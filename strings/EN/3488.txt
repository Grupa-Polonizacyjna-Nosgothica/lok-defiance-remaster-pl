Deep within the Termogent Forest - having successfully navigated its perils - Raziel finds himself before Vorador's Mansion, a private kingdom reputedly as gruesome as it is depraved.

Vorador has earned a reputation for decadence unmatched by any - vampire or human - in Nosgoth's history, but now, all is still. There is no indication of degeneracy - but, thankfully, no sign of the mob of vampire hunters either.

What might Vorador know of Raziel's destiny to enter the Reaver, and how to avert it? To obtain the truth, Raziel must traverse this treacherous, expansive castle, and locate its reclusive host's sanctuary.
