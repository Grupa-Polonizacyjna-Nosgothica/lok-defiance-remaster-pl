Now recovered from the paralytic effects of Moebius's scepter, Kain continues his dogged pursuit of Moebius through the Sarafan headquarters.

Rising from the depths of the Time Streamer's sanctum sanctorum, he finds the Stronghold has descended into chaos. Now alerted to Kain's presence, the Sarafan redouble their efforts to repel his brazen attack, but find themselves falling prey to the same shadowy enemies Moebius unleashed on him.

Kain must regain the upper hand by finding where Moebius has retreated to, and extracting Raziel from the sorcerer's clutches - uncovering more of his own enigmatic destiny as Scion of Balance in the process.
