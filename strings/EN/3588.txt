The Wraith Blade is the final, future incarnation of the Soul Reaver - and the last, eventual manifestation of Raziel's soul in the hellish cycle of his destiny, no longer bound to the vanquished corporeal prison of the sword.

When Kain shattered the Soul Reaver against Raziel, the soul-devouring sentience captive in the blade was released, and bound itself to Raziel as a Wraith Blade, becoming his symbiotic weapon. Unbeknownst to Raziel until the most fateful of moments, this ravenous entity was his own future self - twinned and bound eternally to him.

Kain sought to change history by preventing the Reaver blade from consuming Raziel's soul. Raziel cynically realizes that he can never escape this terrible destiny - Kain has merely postponed it. But why, precisely, is his soul - of all others in Nosgoth - ordained to join with the blade?
