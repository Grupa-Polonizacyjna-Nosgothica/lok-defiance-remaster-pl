In the arcane rooms at the Stronghold's lowest level, a spectral exit leads to a complex of ancient caverns lying directly beneath the Stronghold's foundation. The mass of the Elder God, whose influence spreads throughout Nosgoth, is sprawled across this vast subterranean space.

In the areas nearest the surface, primitive human architecture is also present - crude temples and stones carved with imagery dedicated to the Elder God's worship. As the caverns extend and descend, these human structures become less prolific.

The journey through these seemingly endless abyssal depths is a long trial, hounded by the frightening denizens of the spectral plane.
